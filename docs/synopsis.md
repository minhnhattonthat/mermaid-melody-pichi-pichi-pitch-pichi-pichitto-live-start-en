# Synopsis (for translators and reviewers)

Built from the anime/manga background and a read of the extracted script; the script decides wording. Story mode =
episodes 1-39 (`docs/re/scenes.md`, warp `--var EP=N`), each an intro narration by Lucia (msgs 1281-1329,
"あたし、七海るちあ。" = "I'm Lucia Nanami."), story bubbles, then a song stage ("live") against a villain.

## Who is who
- **Lucia Nanami** (るちあ): mermaid princess of the North Pacific (pink pearl), lives on land as a human girl at the
  Pearl Piari hotel. Cheerful, clumsy, in love with Kaito. Narrates every episode intro.
- **Hanon Hosho** (波音): princess of the South Atlantic (aqua). Lively, teasing, crushes on Mitsuki-sensei.
- **Rina Toin** (リナ): princess of the North Atlantic (green). Cool and mature.
- **Karen** (かれん): princess of the Antarctic (purple), Noelle's twin; refuses to join the others at first ("she won't
  become one of us").
- **Kaito Domoto** (海斗): surfer boy Lucia loves; saved by her as a child. Is (secretly) tied to the sea.
- **Hippo** (ヒッポ): penguin-like attendant, calls Lucia "Lucia-sama".
- **Nikora** (にこら): runs the Pearl Piari hotel, a mermaid herself.
- **Mitsuki-sensei / Taro Mitsuki** (海月せんせい, 太郎): the girls' teacher.
- **Aqua Regina**: the sea goddess.
- **Gaito**: the villain; his minions sing to attack: the **Black Beauty Sisters** (Sheshe, Mimi) and the **Dark
  Lovers** (Izuru, Eriru, Yuri). Maria also appears.
- **Meru** (芽流): a little mermaid looking for her mother; calls the princesses "Princess-sama"-like titles.
- Game characters: Auri, Taki (a fortune-teller: "タキさんのうらない"), Makoto, Momo, Jennifer, Sara (Indian Ocean
  princess, orange).

## Mechanics in the text
- The girls transform with "Pichi Pichi Voice!" and finish with "Love Shower Pitch!"; their pearls sit in pendants.
- "Live" = the song battle/concert; ライブコンサート = live concert; アンコール = encore.
- Episode story also covers trips (a surf contest in the south islands, an ice show, a Christmas party, Kaito's piano
  concert) and minigames.
